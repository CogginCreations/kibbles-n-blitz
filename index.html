How to play

The basics

Teams take turns. On your turn, move your dog, pick a weapon and fire before the timer runs out, then you get a few seconds to retreat. A dog is out when its health hits zero or it falls in the water. The last team with a dog left wins. Wind pushes rockets, so check the wind bar.

Special tools

Sheep: walks and hops on its own; press FIRE / Space / click again to detonate.

Rocket Sheep: press again to launch it, steer with ◀ ▶ / ← → (or drag on touch), press again to explode.

Ninja Rope: fire at a ceiling or wall, swing with ◀ ▶, reel with ▲ ▼, let go with JUMP / Enter / FIRE. You bounce off walls — hold away from a wall as you hit it to kick off harder. Fire again mid-air to re-attach. It doesn't end your turn.

Parachute: open it mid-air (or arm it on the ground, then jump) to float down safely; steer with ◀ ▶.

Minigun: a sweeping burst of bullets — keep aiming while it fires. Baseball Bat: swing at a dog right in front of you to knock it a long way in your aim direction. Holy Hound Grenade: rare crate find; a huge blast after a short heavenly pause.

Air Strike, Teleport, Girder: place with the mouse, a tap or the arrow keys, then confirm with click / FIRE / Space.

Playing with friends online

Choose Online — pass a link on the Play tab. Team 1 goes first, then turns follow the team order; CPU teams play themselves.

When you start the match, copy a personal invite for each friend and text it to them. They open it once and their phone remembers their team. After each turn, tap Copy message and paste it in your group chat — the right player taps the link and goes straight to their turn, everyone else sees who it's waiting on. If someone switches phones or their browser can't remember, they use a PIN instead.

Each link shows a recap of the last turn. Opening an older link than one you've already seen gives a warning, and replaying the same turn shows up in the next recap.

Shopper mode

Played in an indestructible city with a broken girder across the top to swing from. Every team starts with just an unlimited, long-range Ninja Rope and Parachute. A crate drops each turn — rope over, grab it to stock up on weapons, then use them on your rivals.
